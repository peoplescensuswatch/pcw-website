Updates on Census 2027!

 

April 6, 2026:

Emerging reports from several places find that hundreds of teachers across India are missing trainings or seeking exemptions from Census duties amid concerns of rising workload.

As India prepares for its first digital census, a failure to balance challenges faced by overworked teachers and the needed workforce could result in far reaching consequences for the process!

April 1, 2026:

Self-enumeration windows are opening in some states. But how do you actually do it? Visit se.census.gov.in. 

March 31, 2026:

The Houselisting Phase of the Census 2027 officially kicks off on April 1, 2026. This is the first step in ensuring every household is mapped and counted.

You can now log onto the official Census portal to independently submit your household details. To make the process smoother, the Government has released a list of FAQs.
